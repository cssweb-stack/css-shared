## Assessment

The site has localized mixed voice, not a site-wide writing failure. Seven pages
have 11 main-content blocks with first-person department language or mixed reader
address worth editing. The homepage, Why Study, Research Areas landing and five
research detail descriptions follow the current institutional third-person approach;
the Nationalism page separately retains an explicitly labeled source quotation.

The agreed editorial voice is third-person institutional description. Personal
biographies, attributed quotations, source-review excerpts, policy language and
functional instructions need contextual handling, not automatic substitution.
